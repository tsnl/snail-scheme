# Games

Play in your browser. These games are built with the same Lofi libraries described
in the rest of this book.

## Siegework

Build a colony, connect its mines and defenses, and push through the fog to destroy
three enemy nests. Your workers handle the hauling and construction; you decide
where to expand and what to protect.

**[Play Siegework](../play/siegework/)** · [How to play](siegework.md)

Use a current Chromium browser with WebGPU and WasmGC support. Your colony saves
automatically in that browser. The [player guide](siegework.md) covers the opening,
controls, supply network and saving.
