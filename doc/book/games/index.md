# Games

Games are complete `snail-scheme` applications: the rules, the scene and the HUD are
all `snail-scheme`, and the browser only forwards input, draws frames and stores the
save.

- [Siegework](siegework.md): explore a seeded map, wire an energy network
  across it, and destroy three nests before their attacks destroy your hub.
  **[Play it](../play/siegework/)**.
- [Hearsay](https://github.com/tsnl/snail-scheme/blob/main/src/apps/hearsay/README.md):
  a design for a role-playing game about what a city believes, with two
  prototypes to play: the rumor mill and the favor ledger.
  **[Play them](../play/hearsay/)**. Its characters are drawn from
  Liberated Pixel Cup art, credited on the Credits screen of its menu.

- Pick up Rosa in the film crew’s sedan, take her cue on Mercer Street and drive the reel to the editor. [Last Mile](last-mile.md) · [Play](../play/last-mile/).
