# SFIR: complete 57-workload campaign, 15 October 2026

The frozen compiler `8e547bfb353fa5c5aa7c17bc805db13af0d2bca1` completed all
1,956 scheduled attempts: 326 warmups and five samples for each of 326 eligible
routes. All scheduled processes passed their full predicates. SFIR was slower
on the geometric mean in all six target/control groups below. There are faster
individual cases, substantial representation differences and wide variation;
these results establish neither a slowdown's cause nor compiler quality alone.

two Chez timer-name rejections. A separately identified corrected Mperm campaign
completed all six routes and 36 attempts; its results appear separately below.

[packet-input measurements](20261015-input-packets.md) are separate evidence.

per-case ratios, matching-case lists, preparation costs and evidence identities.
The [indexed raw archive](20261015-full57-records.tar.gz) contains original terminal
