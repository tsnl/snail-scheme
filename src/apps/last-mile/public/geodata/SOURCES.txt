Source elevation: U.S. Geological Survey, 3D Elevation Program, 1/3 arc-second bare-earth DEM, tile n38w123, in metres (NAVD88). The game samples it on a 32-metre grid, interpolates across its triangles and models the two bridge decks. Mapped inland lakes each use one DEM waterline. Coastal water is at zero metres in the game.

The baker uses a local metre frame aligned with Lombard Street, clipped to the mainland, Treasure Island, Yerba Buena Island and their bridge approaches. The Golden Gate Bridge extends to its north landing. Building heights and road widths combine mapped tags and estimates. Shopfronts, street markings, kerbs and bridge structures are interpreted geometry. Extra litter, bins and benches are seeded scenery. Traffic, cab stands, fares, people and time of day are game data.

scripts/bake-sf.py rebuilds the database from the three input files. city.json records the input digests, coverage, and the size and location of every derived object. The upstream downloads can change between snapshots.
