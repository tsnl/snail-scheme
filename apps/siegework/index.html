CONNECTING

Field guide

Build a supply line. Put extractors on deposits. Autonomous workers deliver ore, finish construction, and keep the colony running. Add workers as the workload grows.

Survey before expanding. Build on known ground with a route from the hub. Completed towers reveal more terrain; upgrade their range to reach farther. Shaded terrain is remembered; bright terrain is in view.

Hold for eight minutes. Artillery fires automatically. Watch mine stock, work queues, and warnings in field status. Automatic repairs use spare ore while keeping a construction reserve.
