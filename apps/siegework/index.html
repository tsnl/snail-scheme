CONNECTING

First choice: place an extractor on the turquoise diamond, or try Auto · Explore / Defend. Pale cross: hub. Blue mast: observation tower and depot. Outlined sites: construction pending. Rails: solid when active, faint while planned. Shuttles: gold load, hollow when empty.

Field guide

Staff a supply line. Put extractors on deposits. Workers automatically staff each mine's one mining slot; a 40-ore upgrade adds a second slot. Each occupied slot produces at the deposit's rate. Empty slots produce nothing. Other workers carry up to 16 ore per trip to the nearest safe connected depot or hub. A mine holds at most 120 ore. Workers can take damage and flee danger automatically.

Bring ore home. Each tower automatically connects to an earlier completed tower or the hub along known ground. Its single shuttle carries up to 32 ore upstream, then returns empty. Depot stock can accumulate without a capacity limit; growing stock shows a transport bottleneck. Mine and depot stock can supply construction directly; hub arrival adds to your general ore budget. If a rail endpoint is destroyed, its travelling cargo is lost and surviving depots reconnect automatically.

Survey and defend. Build on currently visible ground with a surveyed route from the hub. The tower placement preview shows its proposed rail path; completed towers reveal more terrain. U extends tower sight for 35 ore. T adds artillery for 50 ore while keeping sight and depot service. Rocks block shots. Eastern groups begin at 1:00; outpost raids spawn at 3:30 and 5:30, then the final beast and six escorts at 7:00. The forecast shows the next group before it starts walking toward you.

Shorten construction trips. Workers compare complete delivery times from the hub, extractor buffers and tower stockpiles. One source must hold the entire kit; reserved ore cannot be hauled or put on a train. Once income starts, one mine keeps up to 50 ore for construction while you have three workers and at least 40 hub ore. Loaded workers deliver existing ore first. Cancelling an uncollected kit returns it to its source; a carried kit returns to the hub. There is no automatic repair. Move keeps health and upgrades. Pause preserves planning and freezes work.
