Field guide

Staff a supply line. Put extractors on deposits. Workers automatically staff each mine's one mining slot; a 40-ore upgrade adds a second slot. Each occupied slot produces at the deposit's rate. Empty slots produce nothing. Other workers carry up to 16 ore per trip to the nearest safe connected depot or hub. A mine holds at most 120 ore. Workers can take damage and flee danger automatically.

Bring ore home. Each tower automatically connects to an earlier completed tower or the hub along known ground. Its single shuttle carries up to 32 ore upstream, then returns empty. Depot stock can accumulate without a capacity limit; growing stock shows a transport bottleneck. Mine stock, depot stock and cargo become spendable only on reaching the hub. If a rail endpoint is destroyed, its travelling cargo is lost and surviving depots reconnect automatically.

Survey and defend. Build on currently visible ground with a surveyed route from the hub. The tower placement preview shows its proposed rail path; completed towers reveal more terrain. U extends tower sight for 35 ore. T adds artillery for 50 ore while keeping sight and depot service. All artillery automatically attacks visible threats within eight cells. Shaded terrain is remembered; bright terrain is in view.

Change construction plans. New builds reserve a worker immediately when one can safely take the job. Busy workers settle their cargo before collecting the kit; a gold marker above a worker shows this reservation. C cancels the selected unfinished build. Uncollected kits refund immediately; carried kits must return to the hub first. Automatic repairs keep 40 ore in reserve. Defeated enemies give no ore. Pause leaves camera movement and planning available; construction waits for Resume.
