Field guide

Staff a supply line. Put extractors on deposits. Workers automatically staff each mine's one mining slot; a 40-ore upgrade adds a second slot. Each occupied slot produces at the deposit's rate. Empty slots produce nothing. Other workers haul up to 16 ore per trip to the hub; stored mine ore is not spendable, and a mine holds at most 120. Growing stock means hauling is behind production. Workers can take damage and flee danger automatically.

Survey before expanding. Build on currently visible ground with a surveyed route from the hub. Completed towers reveal more terrain; upgrade their range to reach farther. Shaded terrain is remembered; bright terrain is in view.

Select a site. Hover or move the cursor onto it, then use Upgrade or Prioritize; no click is needed. Artillery automatically attacks visible threats within eight cells. Automatic repairs cover the hub and structures, keeping 40 ore in reserve. Defeated enemies give no ore. Pause stops the shared simulation while allowing camera movement and planning; construction waits for Resume.
